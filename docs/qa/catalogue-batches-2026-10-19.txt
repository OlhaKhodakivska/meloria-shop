Meloria Shop — catalogue batch verification
Date: 19 October 2026

Change
The home-page product lists were truncated to 12 items before reaching the
existing ProductResults component. Removed that truncation for category tabs
and the featured home list. ProductResults now receives the full matching list
and retains its existing 24-item initial batch and load-more behaviour.
Existing category overview cards, new-products limit, styling and API are unchanged.
This reveals products already downloaded in products.json; it is not server pagination.
